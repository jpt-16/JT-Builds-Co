## Projects

`MOBILE DETAILING` · `BEVERLY, MA`

### Clover Downs Detailing

WEBSITE · QUOTE FLOW

A mobile detailer who works on the customer's driveway, so the site is built around one action: send a photo of the car, get a price back. Text and form both land in the same place, and the before-and-after strip uses their own cars rather than stock photography.

[VISIT SITE →](https://cloverdownsdetailing.com)

`CONCEPT BUILD` · `BAR & RESTAURANT` · `SOMERS POINT, NJ`

### Charlie's Bar & Restaurant

WEBSITE

A corner bar that has held the same Somers Point corner since 1944, now run by the fourth generation. Eighty years of history, two sets of opening hours, menus, parties, and directions — arranged so a first-time visitor finds the wings and the door without hunting for them. Built as a proposal; not commissioned.

[VIEW THE BUILD →](https://charlies-bar.vercel.app)

`CONCEPT BUILD` · `ATHLETE RECRUITING` · `MA · NH · NC`

### GSGR

WEBSITE

A recruiting and training service for high-school athletes, where the parent reading the page is the one deciding. Training, film evaluation, athlete branding and recruiting guidance are separated out so each is easy to understand on its own, and every route through the page ends at the same application form. Built as a proposal; not commissioned.

[VIEW THE BUILD →](https://getseengetresults.vercel.app)
