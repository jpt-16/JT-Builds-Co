Fees & Payment

An engagement is a single recurring monthly fee. It covers designing and building the site, hosting it, security, backups, technical maintenance, and minor content updates. There is no separate setup or build charge: the build is recovered through the monthly fee rather than billed before work starts. Substantial work beyond that — redesigns, additional pages, or new features — is quoted separately and agreed before it is carried out. Optional add-ons such as branding, booking, local search, and advertising are quoted on their own. Where we set up or run advertising, our fee covers that work only: the advertising budget itself is paid by you to Google or Meta directly, is set by you, and is not part of what you pay us. Exact pricing and payment terms are quoted and agreed during your consultation and confirmed in writing before work begins.

The fee covers hosting, security, backups and technical maintenance, so that none of it falls to you to manage. It also covers minor content updates such as new photos, revised copy, pricing, and hours, carried out by us on request. Larger work, such as a redesign, additional pages, or new functionality, falls outside the monthly fee and is quoted separately before it is undertaken.
