Our Services

Fees & Payment

An engagement has two parts: a one-time setup fee for the initial build, and a monthly Care plan covering hosting, security, backups, technical maintenance, and minor content updates. Both are required for a site we build and host, because a live website needs somewhere to run and someone to look after it. Substantial work beyond that — redesigns, additional pages, or new features — is quoted separately and agreed before it is carried out. Optional add-ons such as branding, booking, and local search are quoted on their own. Exact pricing and payment terms are quoted and agreed during your consultation and confirmed in writing before work begins.

The 48-Hour Promise

Once you sign off on the agreed plan, delivery of the initial build is completed within 48 hours. If we're late, the setup fee is waived. This promise covers the website build itself; it doesn't cover delays caused by waiting on content, feedback, or approvals from you. Custom work agreed as a separate add-on runs to its own timeline, agreed with that piece of work.

Services that send text messages on your behalf — such as missed-call text-back and automated review requests — additionally require carrier verification of your business number before they can go live. That registration is reviewed by the phone carriers, not by us, and typically takes anywhere from a few days to a few weeks. We file it at sign-off; the build is still delivered within the promise, and messaging activates once the carriers approve it.

Ownership & Care

Your website belongs to your business. Once the setup fee is paid, the design, the written content, the images you supply or we produce for you, and your domain are yours. We do not claim ownership of them.

Care is a service, not a claim on the site. Where you engage us for ongoing Care, we host and maintain the website on your behalf — keeping it online, secure, and backed up — so that none of it falls to you to manage. Care also covers minor content updates such as new photos, revised copy, pricing, and hours, carried out by us on request. Larger work, such as a redesign, additional pages, or new functionality, falls outside Care and is quoted separately before it is undertaken.

Your Google Business Profile is handled however you prefer: we can set it up and hand you the login, or manage it as part of your Care plan. Specific access and handover arrangements for any engagement are confirmed with you directly.

Because Care is what hosts and maintains the site, cancelling it means we stop hosting your website at the end of the period you've paid for. What you own is unaffected: your content, images, and domain remain yours, and we'll work with you to hand them over so you can host the site elsewhere if you'd prefer.
